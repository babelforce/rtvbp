Each SVG is a lossless wrapper around the unmodified source PNG so it can be reviewed and committed
as text. The source image remains byte-identical inside the wrapper. Current colors (`#05445e`,
`#f94721`, `#5bcace`, `#181e32`, and `#ecf8fd`) and the DM Sans/Spectral font pairing were observed
from [the babelforce homepage](https://www.babelforce.com/) on 2026-08-14. The short steward-card
line “Voice, built for change” is the homepage's current heading.

was identified on the source site; their inclusion here is limited to an official babelforce
project's documentation and does not grant downstream trademark rights. This provenance note does
not alter the licensing of any protocol source or prose in the repository.
